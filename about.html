My story

Why accounting, and why I teach

A short first-person story — from the ledger to the classroom.

I chose accounting because I like turning complexity into something managers can actually use. Numbers only matter when they help people make better decisions — about cash, risk, or growth.

Over the last three years I’ve worked inside trading and factory environments. Most recently, as Accountant at BHAS Pvt. Ltd. / Hulas Steel Unit-3, I managed a Rs 7.59 Crore receivables portfolio, built weekly liquidity snapshots and cash-flow forecasts, tightened order-to-cash controls with pre-numbered loading slips, and handled full-cycle VAT and TDS compliance. The same habits that keep a factory’s cash flowing also make exam answers stronger.

That’s why I teach. At British Management College I deliver Financial Accounting and Audit & Assurance to ACCA and CA students, always connecting the syllabus to how controls and reporting actually look in Nepali businesses. Theory is useful; theory that students can recognise in a real ledger is better.
