ACCA & CA in Nepal: Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on ICAN, ACCA, audit rights, exemptions, exam structure and accounting rules in Nepal.

Last reviewed: 3 November 2026

About ACCA and ICAN

What is ACCA?

ACCA is the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants, a global professional body. Members who complete the exams, the ethics module and the required practical experience earn the Chartered Certified Accountant designation.

What is ICAN and what does it do?

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nepal (ICAN) was set up under the Nepal Chartered Accountants Act, 1997. It is Nepal's national accounting body and regulator: it runs the Chartered Accountant (CA) exams, issues membership certificates and certificates of practice, and handles discipline for its members.

Can an ACCA member sign statutory audit reports in Nepal?

Statutory audit practice in Nepal is regulated by ICAN, so an ACCA qualification on its own does not give the right to sign statutory audit reports. ACCA members who want that right generally need to qualify with ICAN as well. Many ACCA members build careers in internal audit, financial reporting, tax, risk and advisory work, where ICAN practice rights are not required. Rules can change, so check current requirements with ICAN.

Is there a route for ACCA members to become ICAN members?

ICAN has announced a "CA Membership Examination for ACCAs" in recent years, including a round with forms due for December 2026. Eligibility, syllabus, fees and dates are set by ICAN, so read its latest notice at ican.org.np.

Is ACCA better than CA?

It depends on your goal. ACCA is recognised internationally and can be studied with flexible exam sittings, which suits people planning to work or move abroad. The CA qualification from ICAN is the route to practising as a statutory auditor in Nepal. Many professionals eventually hold both. Choose based on where you want to work and which roles you want.

Studying ACCA

How is the ACCA qualification structured?

ACCA has three levels. Applied Knowledge covers Business and Technology (BT), Management Accounting (MA) and Financial Accounting (FA). Applied Skills covers Corporate and Business Law (LW), Performance Management (PM), Taxation (TX), Financial Reporting (FR), Audit and Assurance (AA) and Financial Management (FM). Strategic Professional has two essentials, Strategic Business Leader (SBL) and Strategic Business Reporting (SBR), plus two options chosen from AFM, APM, ATX and AAA. You also complete the Ethics and Professional Skills module and 36 months of approved practical experience. ACCA updates its rules from time to time, so confirm details at accaglobal.com.

What are exemptions?

ACCA may exempt you from some papers if you already hold certain degrees, diplomas or professional qualifications. Exemptions are decided by ACCA, and a fee is charged per exempted paper. Use the exemption calculator on the ACCA website to see what you may qualify for.

How long does ACCA take?

It varies a lot. The time depends on how many papers you are exempt from, how many you attempt per sitting, whether you pass first time, and how quickly you complete the practical experience. Many students take several years, so plan for a steady pace instead of a fixed deadline.

Where can I practise exam-style questions?

Accounting and tax in Nepal

What are NFRS?

Nepal Financial Reporting Standards (NFRS) are the financial reporting standards used in Nepal, based on IFRS and issued by the Accounting Standards Board. The ACCA Financial Reporting and Strategic Business Reporting papers are based on IFRS, so it helps to know where NFRS differs. See the NFRS and IFRS reference guide.

When does the Nepali fiscal year start?

It starts on Shrawan 1 in mid-July and ends at the end of Asar. Fiscal year 2083/84 began on Shrawan 1, 2083, about 17 July 2026. Use the B.S. to A.D. converter to check any date.

What are the personal income tax slabs for FY 2083/84?

For resident individuals: 1% up to Rs 10 lakh, 10% to Rs 15 lakh, 20% to Rs 25 lakh, 27% to Rs 40 lakh and 29% above Rs 40 lakh, with the same table for single and married taxpayers. Try the Nepal income tax calculator, and confirm the current rules with the Inland Revenue Department.
