Why accountants need it

  • Nepali tax, VAT and company filings are dated in B.S., and the fiscal year runs Shrawan to Asar.
  • Loan, lease and depreciation schedules often mix B.S. and A.D. dates. Convert first, then use the depreciation calculator or EMI calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How far apart are Bikram Sambat (B.S.) and A.D. years?

B.S. runs about 56 years and 8 months ahead of the A.D. calendar. Baishakh 1, the Nepali new year, falls in mid-April, so dates before it are 57 years ahead and dates after it are 56 years ahead.

Why does a B.S. date conversion need a lookup table?

B.S. months have between 29 and 32 days, and the lengths change from year to year. There is no simple formula, so converters use published month-length tables. This one covers B.S. 2000 to 2090 (April 1943 to April 2034 A.D.).

When does the Nepali fiscal year start?

The fiscal year begins on Shrawan 1, in mid-July, and ends at the end of Asar. For example, fiscal year 2083/84 began on Shrawan 1, 2083, which is about 17 July 2026.

Can I rely on this for legal or official documents?

Use it for planning and quick checks. For legal documents, citizenship records or court deadlines, confirm the date with the official calendar or the issuing office, because calendar tables can differ by a day in rare cases.
