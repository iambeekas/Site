Area D · Inventories

Inventory valuation (FIFO & AVCO)

Closing inventory affects both cost of sales and the SFP. FA tests FIFO and weighted average (AVCO), and the lower of cost and NRV rule.

Learning outcomes

  • Value inventory using FIFO and periodic weighted average
  • Apply lower of cost and net realisable value (NRV)
  • Show the effect on gross profit when closing inventory changes

Core rules

FIFO: issues and closing stock assumed from oldest purchases first.

AVCO (periodic): weighted average unit cost = total cost of goods available ÷ total units available; apply to issues and closing stock.

IAS 2: measure at lower of cost and NRV. NRV = estimated selling price − costs to complete and sell.

Worked sketch

Opening 10 @ Rs. 5; purchase 20 @ Rs. 6; sold 18. FIFO closing 12 units = 2 @ 5 + 10 @ 6 = Rs. 70. AVCO unit cost = (50+120)/30 = Rs. 5.67; closing ≈ Rs. 68.

Common exam traps

  • Including abnormal waste or selling costs in cost (they are not inventory cost).
  • Writing inventory down and forgetting that a later NRV recovery can reverse (within limits) — FA often sticks to simple write-down.
  • Mixing continuous and periodic AVCO methods in one answer.

Quick quiz

1. Cost Rs. 80, NRV Rs. 70. Inventory figure in SFP?
Rs. 70 — lower of cost and NRV. Write-down Rs. 10 is an expense.
