Statement of Cash Flows

Automatic calculation with editable inputs

The statement now derives opening cash, depreciation, impairment and working-capital movements from SOPL and current/comparative SOFP balances where available. For a first reporting year with no comparative balances, opening balances default to zero. You can override any derived amount below. Enter actual tax paid and material investing / financing transactions that cannot safely be inferred from closing balances.

Apply classifications consistently and assess the IFRS 18 requirements where the entity has specified main business activities. These policy selections are not a substitute for that assessment.
