Financial Statement Generator

Prepare your financial reporting data from an uploaded trial balance or enter it manually if you are a small business without formal accounting records.

🔒Your data stays in this browser. This workspace saves drafts on this device and does not upload your financial figures to our server.

Enter business figures

Add only the categories that apply

Income

Enter current-period amounts and optional comparative figures.

Expenses

Enter expenses using positive amounts. Add separate lines where useful.

Assets and liabilities

Enter closing balances at the reporting date. These are not cash movements.

Comparative closing balances (optional)

Enter the previous reporting date balances if comparative statements are required.

Owner's capital and drawings

For a sole trader, the capital reconciliation is calculated from these inputs and the profit figure above.

Partners' capital and drawings

Enter aggregate partner capital movements for this initial version. Detailed partner-by-partner schedules will follow.

Equity movements

Companies must provide material movements; the tool will not infer share issues, dividends or reserve transfers from summary figures.

A full company Statement of Changes in Equity requires movements by equity component. This initial input screen captures summary movements only; detailed reserve and OCI schedules will be added in the next build stage.

04

Account classification and mapping

Review every suggested classification before approval

Suggestions are based on account descriptions only. They are not accounting conclusions. Confirm the underlying transactions and entity-specific circumstances before approving a mapping.

Provide figures and prepare mappings to begin.

For IFRS 18, operating, investing and financing categories depend on the applicable requirements and, for certain items, the entity's specified main business activities. This mapping screen is an initial classification aid, not an IFRS compliance determination.

07

Statement of Cash Flows

Automatic calculation with editable inputs

The statement now derives opening cash, depreciation, impairment and working-capital movements from SOPL and current/comparative SOFP balances where available. For a first reporting year with no comparative balances, opening balances default to zero. You can override any derived amount below. Enter actual tax paid and material investing / financing transactions that cannot safely be inferred from closing balances.

Apply classifications consistently and assess the IFRS 18 requirements where the entity has specified main business activities. These policy selections are not a substitute for that assessment.

Statement of Changes in Equity

Component-wise reconciliation

Enter opening balances and movements for each equity component. Enter profit allocated to equity and OCI movements separately; do not include the same profit or OCI twice. Distributions and drawings are entered as positive amounts and deducted automatically.
