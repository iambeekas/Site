Audit & assurance

Substantive testing checklist

Support notes for accountants who prepare schedules for auditors or perform internal substantive checks.

Assertions — keep them on the working paper

Assertion (simplified)Question you are answering
Existence / occurrenceDid this balance or transaction really happen?
CompletenessIs anything missing that should be recorded?
Accuracy / valuationAre amounts correct and valued properly?
Rights & obligationsDoes the entity own it / owe it?
Cut-offIs it in the right period?
PresentationIs classification and disclosure fair?

High-yield substantive ideas by area

  • Bank: reconcile to statement; test reconciling items to clearing.
  • Receivables: after-date cash, confirmations where used, allowance rationale.
  • Payables: supplier statements, after-date invoices for completeness.
  • Inventory: count attendance results, NRV tests on aged lines.
  • PPE: additions to invoice, disposals, depreciation recalculation.
  • Revenue: sequence tests, cut-off around period end, unusual journals.

Working paper discipline

  • Source, date, preparer, reviewer initials.
  • Sample method stated (not only “selected items”).
  • Exceptions listed with conclusion (adjusted / accepted / escalated).
