How the tax is worked out

Nepal uses a progressive system. You first work out taxable income (total income minus allowed deductions), then apply each rate only to the part of income that falls inside its band. The calculator shows this band by band in the table above.

Worked examples

  • Taxable income Rs 18,00,000, 1% applies: Rs 10,000 + Rs 50,000 + Rs 60,000 (20% of Rs 3,00,000) = Rs 1,20,000.
  • Taxable income Rs 15,00,000, 1% waived: nothing on the first Rs 10 lakh, then 10% of Rs 5,00,000 = Rs 50,000.
  • Taxable income Rs 60,00,000, 1% applies: Rs 6,65,000 up to Rs 40 lakh, plus 29% of Rs 20,00,000 (Rs 5,80,000) = Rs 12,45,000.

Frequently asked questions

What are the income tax slabs in Nepal for FY 2083/84?

For resident individuals the same table applies to single and married taxpayers: 1% on income up to Rs 10 lakh, 10% from Rs 10 to 15 lakh, 20% from Rs 15 to 25 lakh, 27% from Rs 25 to 40 lakh, and 29% above Rs 40 lakh. Each rate applies only to the slice of income inside its band.

Which fiscal year do these slabs apply to?

They apply to income for FY 2083/84, which started on Shrawan 1, 2083 (about 17 July 2026). Income for earlier years is assessed under the slabs that applied in that year, so do not use this calculator for old returns.

What is the 1% on the first Rs 10 lakh?

The first band is charged at 1% as Social Security Tax. It is reported to be waived for contributors to the Social Security Fund (SSF) or an approved retirement fund, and for some other categories such as pension income. Use the dropdown to see both results, and confirm your own position with your employer or the Inland Revenue Department.

What should I enter as deductions?

Enter the total of deductions that the law allows you in a year, such as qualifying retirement fund contributions, insurance premiums or medical expenses, up to their legal limits. The calculator subtracts the amount you type and does not check limits, so check the current limits first.

Does this calculate employer-side or business taxes?

No. It covers personal income tax on a resident individual's taxable income only. It does not cover companies, VAT, TDS on other payments, capital gains, or non-residents.
